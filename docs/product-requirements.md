# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - Ladies Only Events

## Overview
A luxury bilingual (Arabic/English) web platform for exclusive women-only trips and community gatherings in Egypt and the region, managed by Dalia & Rania.

## Key Features
1. **Bilingual Experience & RTL Support:** Fully optimized for Arabic and English users[cite: 1].
2. **Visual Identity:** Elegant feminine color palette (Burgundy `#7A1C3E`, Soft Rose, Peach, Ivory) with Playfair Display & Cairo typography[cite: 1].
3. **Manual Payment & Verification:** InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, and Bank Transfer with receipt upload and instant admin verification[cite: 1].
4. **Automated WhatsApp Notifications:** Instant ticket dispatch, QR codes, and OTP login via Meta WhatsApp Cloud API.
5. **Admin Control Panel:** Financial KPIs, bookings management, customer CRM, and marketing analytics[cite: 1].
